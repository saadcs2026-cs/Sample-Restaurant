A neighbourhood café built on three simple things: good coffee, honest food, and a warm welcome.
Founded in 2024, The Green Leaf started as a small kitchen with one espresso machine and a big idea — that a café should feel like a friend's living room. We roast our own beans, bake our own bread, and source produce from farms within 100 km of Lahore. Whether you're here for a quick flat white or a slow Sunday brunch, we're glad you're here.
